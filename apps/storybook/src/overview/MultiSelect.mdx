## Overview

Space toggles a row and the menu stays open. Escape or Tab closes it.

<StoryWithCode of={Stories.Default} />

## Sizes

The field comes in large and small, empty and with chips. The chips are xsmall in both sizes.

<StoryWithCode of={Stories.Sizes} />

## States

The field can be enabled, complete, with helper text, in error, or disabled empty and disabled complete. Disabled chips have no remove button.

<StoryWithCode of={Stories.States} />

## Overflow

When there are more chips than fit, the rest become one "+N" chip.

<StoryWithCode of={Stories.Overflow} />

## Searchable

A search field in the menu header filters the rows. Down moves into them. Backspace in an empty search removes the last chip.

<StoryWithCode of={Stories.Searchable} />

## With descriptions

Options can have a description line. Options can also be disabled.

<StoryWithCode of={Stories.WithDescriptions} />

## Controlled

The page holds the chosen values.

<StoryWithCode of={Stories.Controlled} />

## Accessibility

- The field is a combobox with `aria-haspopup="listbox"` and `aria-expanded`. The menu is a listbox with `aria-multiselectable="true"`.
- Space toggles the focused option. The menu stays open while you choose. Escape or Tab closes it.
- Each chip's remove button comes after the field in the tab order, for example "Remove Tier 2".
- The "+N" chip is announced as "and N more". Activating it opens the menu.
- Searching filters the rows. "No results" is announced politely.
