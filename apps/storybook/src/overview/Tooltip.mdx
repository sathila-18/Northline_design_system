## Accessibility

- It appears on hover, after a short delay, and on keyboard focus of the trigger. It disappears on pointer leave, blur or Escape.
- Hovering the tooltip itself keeps it open.
- It has `role="tooltip"`, and the trigger gets `aria-describedby`. On an icon-only button the tooltip repeats the hidden label, so it stays a description and the name comes from the button.
- A rich tooltip describes the trigger with both the heading and the description. It holds no interactive content.
- It fades in and out. With reduced motion it doesn't fade.
- Touch has no hover, so the information must also be available another way.
