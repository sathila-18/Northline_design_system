## Accessibility

- The visible count is short. Give its meaning through `label`, for example "3 unread notifications". The label is visually hidden text, and the digits are hidden from screen readers.
- With no label and no count (the dot), the badge is decorative.
- There is no live region by default. A parent that needs count changes announced adds its own.
- White text on the warning tone in Light needs 4.5 to 1 in every brand.
