## Labelled

With `label`, the icon has `role="img"` and an accessible name. Use it when no text nearby gives the meaning.

<StoryWithCode of={Stories.Labelled} />

## Accessibility

- It is decorative by default, because the text next to it carries the meaning. With `label`, the icon has `role="img"` and that name.
- Icons on their tints need 3 to 1 in every brand and theme.
