## Accessibility

- It is a `nav` named "Pagination" that holds a list.
- Previous and next are named "Previous page" and "Next page". At the ends they are disabled and can't be focused.
- The current page has `aria-current="page"`. After a page change, focus moves to the new current page button. If previous or next was used, focus stays there.
- With one page, previous and next are both disabled and one page item shows.
