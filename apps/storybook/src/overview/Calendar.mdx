## Accessibility

- The days form a grid labelled with the month and year. The weekday headers use the full day names as their text.
- The grid is one tab stop. Arrow keys move by a day or a week, into the next or previous month.
- Home and End go to the start and end of the week. Page Up and Page Down go to the previous and next month. With Shift, they go to the previous and next year.
- Enter or Space selects. In range mode the first choice sets the start and the second sets the end.
- The previous and next buttons are named "Previous month" and "Next month".
- The month and year title is a polite live region, so a change of month is announced.
- Disabled dates are announced as unavailable. They can't be selected but stay reachable by arrow keys.
