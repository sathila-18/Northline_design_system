## Accessibility

- It sits in a `header`, which the app shell provides. The title is the page's `h1`.
- The back button is named "Back", or "Back to" followed by the page. It is a link when `backHref` is set.
- A cut title keeps its full text available.
- New notifications are announced politely from here, for example "3 new notifications".
