## Accessibility

- Without pages, it is a link with `aria-current="page"` when it is the current page.
- With pages, it is a `button` with `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` that shows the pages, which are links. A section holding the current page opens by default.
- Minimized, the label stays as visually hidden text and shows in a tooltip. A section with pages opens them in a menu.
- Tab moves through rows and open pages. Enter or Space opens or closes a section.
