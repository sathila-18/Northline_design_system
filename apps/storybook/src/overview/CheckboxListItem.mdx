A body can replace the description. `showBody` turns the body or description off.

<StoryWithCode of={Stories.Body} />

## Accessibility

- The whole row toggles. It is a `label` around a native checkbox.
- Space toggles. The focus ring goes around the row, not the box.
- A group of rows takes a group label (`role="group"` with a name), for example "Tiers". The page provides the wrapper.
