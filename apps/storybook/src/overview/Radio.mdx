## Accessibility

- It is a native radio input, visually replaced. The native input keeps the semantics and keyboard.
- Tab moves into the group, to the selected radio or the first one. Arrow keys move and select within the group. Space selects the focused radio.
- Keyboard focus shows the standard focus ring around the 24 × 24 box.
- A disabled radio uses native `disabled` and can't be focused.
- A screen reader hears the name, "radio button", the state and the position, for example "Tier 2, radio button, not selected, 2 of 3".
- The ring colour on the page background must reach 3 to 1.
