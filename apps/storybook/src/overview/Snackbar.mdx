## Live

`NlSnackbarProvider` and `useNlSnackbar` show one snackbar at a time. A new one replaces the old. Without an action it closes after 6 seconds. With an action it stays. Press F6 to move focus into it.

## Accessibility

- It shows in a live region that exists from page load. Neutral and success use `role="status"`. Warning and error use `role="alert"`.
- It doesn't take focus. F6, or the next Tab, moves into the snackbar region.
- A snackbar with an action doesn't close by itself. It stays until closed or acted on. Without an action it closes after its duration, and pauses while hovered or focused.
- It enters and leaves with a fade and slide. With reduced motion it only fades.
- One shows at a time. A new one replaces the old.
