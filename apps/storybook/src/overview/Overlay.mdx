## Accessibility

- It is always hidden from screen readers. It is never focusable and has no name.
- The parent owns focus trapping, Escape to close and making the page behind inert. The overlay only draws the dim layer and passes clicks to the parent.
- It fades in and out. With reduced motion it appears and disappears with no fade.
- The parent locks page scrolling while the overlay shows.
