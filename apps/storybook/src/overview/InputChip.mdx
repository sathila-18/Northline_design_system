## Overview

Tab to the remove button. Enter, Space, Backspace or Delete removes the chip.

<StoryWithCode of={Stories.Default} />

## Sizes

There are three sizes. The remove button's 24 × 24 target doesn't change the chip's size.

<StoryWithCode of={Stories.Sizes} />

## With leading

A chip can have a leading icon.

<StoryWithCode of={Stories.WithLeading} />

## Disabled

A disabled chip is greyed, with no border, shadow or remove button.

<StoryWithCode of={Stories.Disabled} />

## Removable list

A list of chosen values. Removing one moves focus to the next chip's remove button, or the previous one at the end. The caller moves the focus.

<StoryWithCode of={Stories.RemovableList} />

## Accessibility

- The chip itself isn't interactive. It is a `span` with the label and a remove `button`.
- The remove button is named "Remove" followed by the label, for example "Remove Tier 2". Enter or Space removes.
- Backspace or Delete on the focused remove button also removes.
- A disabled chip has no remove button.
- The remove icon is 16. Its button is at least 24 × 24 without changing the chip's look.
