## Accessibility

- It is a native `button` with `aria-pressed`. Space or Enter toggles it.
- A screen reader hears the label, "toggle button", and "pressed" or "not pressed", for example "Active members, toggle button, pressed".
- A disabled chip uses native `disabled` and can't be focused.
