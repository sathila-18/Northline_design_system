## Overview

One header cell inside a table row. Try the controls to see every option.

<StoryWithCode of={Stories.Default} />

## Appearances

Rows can be plain or grey. Titles can be plain or sortable, and can have a filter.

<StoryWithCode of={Stories.Appearances} />

## Sorting

Click a title to sort. It cycles from none to ascending to descending and back to none, one column at a time.

<StoryWithCode of={Stories.Sorting} />

## Filters

A filter can be available, open or applied. Click it to open or close.

<StoryWithCode of={Stories.Filters} />

## Narrow and select all

A narrow select-all column with its title hidden, and a narrow row-actions column.

<StoryWithCode of={Stories.NarrowAndSelectAll} />

## Accessibility

- It is a column header (`th scope="col"`) with `aria-sort` when sortable.
- The sort button is named by the column. The table announces the new order, for example "Sorted by points, ascending". The cell doesn't.
- The filter button is named "Filter" followed by the column, with ", applied" when a filter is on. It has `aria-haspopup="dialog"` and `aria-expanded`.
- The select-all checkbox is named "Select all rows". It is indeterminate when some rows are selected.
