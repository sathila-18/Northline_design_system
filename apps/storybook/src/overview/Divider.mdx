## Decorative

`decorative` renders a hidden `div` in place of an `hr`, for lines that are only visual. It looks the same.

<StoryWithCode of={Stories.Decorative} />

## Accessibility

- It renders `hr`, which has `role="separator"`. A screen reader may announce "separator".
- `decorative` renders a hidden `div` instead, for lines that are only visual, such as between list rows or inside menus.
