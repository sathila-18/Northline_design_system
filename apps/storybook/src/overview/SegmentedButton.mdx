## Overview

Tab in, then use the arrow keys, Home and End to change the selection.

<StoryWithCode of={Stories.Default} />

## Sizes

There are three sizes. Labelled tracks fill their container and the segments share the width.

<StoryWithCode of={Stories.Sizes} />

## Two and three options

A segmented button has two or three options. Each option can have an icon.

<StoryWithCode of={Stories.TwoAndThree} />

## Icon only

With `iconOnly`, the track hugs its segments. Each label becomes the segment's hidden name and shows in a tooltip.

<StoryWithCode of={Stories.IconOnly} />

## Disabled

`disabled` turns off the whole group. Every segment uses its disabled look.

<StoryWithCode of={Stories.Disabled} />

## Controlled

The page holds the value. This example is a theme switch.

<StoryWithCode of={Stories.Controlled} />

## Accessibility

- It is a radio group with a name. Each segment is a radio.
- It is one tab stop, on the selected segment. Arrow keys move and select, wrapping at the ends. Home and End go to the first and last.
- Icon-only segments keep their label as a hidden name and show it in a tooltip.
