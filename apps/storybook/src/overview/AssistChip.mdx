## Overview

Hover, press and Tab to the chip to see its states.

<StoryWithCode of={Stories.Default} />

## Action

Without `dropdown`, the chip has no arrow. It runs something when clicked.

<StoryWithCode of={Stories.Action} />

## Dropdown

With `dropdown`, the chip opens something. It can be closed, open or disabled. Open uses the selected look and an up arrow.

<StoryWithCode of={Stories.Dropdown} />

## Sizes

There are three sizes, closed and open.

<StoryWithCode of={Stories.Sizes} />

## With a menu

Click the chip or press Down to open the menu. Choosing a range closes the menu and updates the label. Escape closes it and returns focus to the chip.

<StoryWithCode of={Stories.WithMenu} />

## Accessibility

- It is a native `button`. Enter or Space activates it.
- With `dropdown`, it has `aria-haspopup` matching what it opens, `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`. Down also opens the menu. Escape in the menu returns focus to the chip.
- A screen reader hears, for example, "Date range, button, collapsed, has popup".
