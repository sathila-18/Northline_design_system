## Overview

An optional note sits above. A tertiary button sits at the start. Secondary and primary buttons sit at the end.

<StoryWithCode of={Stories.Default} />

## Combinations

The usual sets are primary only, secondary and primary, tertiary and primary, and a note with a primary.

<StoryWithCode of={Stories.Combinations} />

## Sticky

The container pins the dock to the bottom of a scrolling panel. The dock then shows the sticky shadow.

<StoryWithCode of={Stories.Sticky} />

## In a form

In a form, the primary button submits the form.

<StoryWithCode of={Stories.InForm} />

## Narrow

In a narrow container the end group wraps and stays at the end.

<StoryWithCode of={Stories.Narrow} />

## Destructive

The secondary button can be destructive, with a leading icon. A details side sheet uses this.

<StoryWithCode of={Stories.Destructive} />

## Accessibility

- The DOM order matches the visual order: tertiary, secondary, primary. Tab follows it.
- When the dock is sticky, the scrolling content above has bottom padding, so nothing hides behind the dock.
- In a form, the primary button is the submit button (`type="submit"`).
