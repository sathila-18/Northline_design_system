## Accessibility

- It is a native range input, styled. A screen reader hears the name, "slider" and the value, for example "Points, slider, 50".
- Left or Down takes off one step. Right or Up adds one. Page Down and Page Up move 10% of the range. Home and End go to the minimum and maximum.
- `aria-valuetext` can add a unit, for example "50 points".
- A disabled slider uses native `disabled`. It can't be focused and takes no pointer events.
- Colour changes on hover and press don't animate when reduced motion is on.
- The fill and thumb need 3 to 1 against the track in every brand and theme.
