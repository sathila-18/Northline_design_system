## Accessibility

- It has `role="dialog"` and `aria-modal="true"`, and the title labels it.
- On open, focus moves into the sheet, to the first field or the close button. Focus is trapped and returns to the trigger on close.
- Escape and the close button close it. A click on the overlay closes it unless there are unsaved changes, which the caller confirms.
- The back button returns to the previous step inside the sheet. It doesn't close it.
- It slides in from the right. With reduced motion it only fades.
- The page behind is inert and doesn't scroll.
