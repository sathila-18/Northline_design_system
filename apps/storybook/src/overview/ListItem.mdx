## Accessory align

`accessoryAlign` centres the leading icon and the chevron on the row (the default) or puts them at the top, on the title's first line. Use the top for cards with several lines, such as reward cards.

## Accessibility

- As a link or button, the whole row is the control. Its name is the row's text: the title, then the description and caption.
- Inside a menu, it has `role="menuitem"` (action menus) or `role="option"` with `aria-selected` (select menus).
- Outside menus, the page sets `aria-current` on a selected row. `selected` is only the look.
- A static row has no hover and no focus. With a chevron, the whole row is clickable.
- The chevron and the leading icon are decorative.
