## Accessibility

- It has `role="progressbar"` with a range of 0 to 100, `aria-valuenow` and a name from `label` or `aria-labelledby`.
- A screen reader hears, for example, "Upload, progress bar, 50%".
- With reduced motion the width jumps with no animation.
- The fill needs 3 to 1 against the track in every brand and theme.
