## Accessibility

- A banner present when the page loads has no role. It is just content in the page.
- A banner added after load uses `role="status"` for info, success and neutral, or `role="alert"` for error and warning, so it is announced.
- The close button is named "Dismiss", or "Dismiss" followed by the title. After closing, the caller moves focus to a sensible place.
- Colour isn't the only signal. The icon and the text carry the meaning.
