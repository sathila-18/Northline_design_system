## Overview

Focus the field to see the pattern. Type digits and the slashes are added, or open the calendar.

<StoryWithCode of={Stories.Default} />

## States

The field can be enabled, complete, with helper text, in error, or disabled empty and disabled complete.

<StoryWithCode of={Stories.States} />

## Limits

`minDate` and `maxDate` disable days outside them in the calendar. Typing one shows an error.

<StoryWithCode of={Stories.Limits} />

## Errors

Dates that are invalid or out of range show their error. Type 31/02/2026 to see "Enter a date as DD/MM/YYYY".

<StoryWithCode of={Stories.Errors} />

## Formats

`format` sets the pattern. DD/MM/YYYY is the default. Month first and ISO order with dashes also work.

<StoryWithCode of={Stories.Formats} />

## Controlled

The page holds the value as an ISO date.

<StoryWithCode of={Stories.Controlled} />

## Accessibility

- It is a text input with a digit mask. Slashes are added automatically.
- Invalid or out-of-range dates show an error, for example "Enter a date as DD/MM/YYYY" or "Choose a date from" followed by the earliest date.
- The calendar button is named "Choose date", or "Change date" followed by the current date. It has `aria-haspopup="dialog"` and `aria-expanded`.
- The calendar menu is a dialog. Choosing a date fills the field, closes the menu and returns focus to the input. Escape closes it.
- `aria-describedby` includes the format hint, so screen reader users know the pattern.
