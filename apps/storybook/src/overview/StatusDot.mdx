## Accessibility

- Colour alone never carries meaning. Where presence matters, pair the dot with a text status nearby, such as "Online", or give it a `label`.
- It is decorative by default and hidden from screen readers.
- With `label`, it has `role="img"` and that name. A screen reader hears, for example, "Online".
- The dot needs 3 to 1 against the ring and the avatar, in every brand and theme.
