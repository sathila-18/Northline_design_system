## Accessibility

- It is a native `button` with `role="switch"` and `aria-checked`.
- Tab moves to it. Space or Enter toggles it.
- A screen reader hears the name, "switch", and "on" or "off", for example "Email updates, switch, on".
- With reduced motion, the handle doesn't slide and colours don't animate.
- A disabled switch uses native `disabled` and can't be focused.
- The off track on the page background, and the white handle on both tracks, need 3 to 1 in every brand and theme.
