## Accessibility

- The field is a combobox with `aria-haspopup="listbox"`, `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`, named by the label. Its text is the current value.
- Enter, Space or Down opens. Arrows move. Enter selects and closes. Escape closes without a change. A letter jumps to the next option that starts with it, on the closed field too.
- It doesn't use a native `select`, so the menu can match the design. A hidden input with `name` submits the value.
- The selected option has `aria-selected="true"`. Disabled options are skipped.
